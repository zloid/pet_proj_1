Status: Complete

Purpose:
Connect the emulator core to the actual Bangle.js application experience.

Implemented in apps/gbemu/app.js:
- Selecting a ROM creates Core.create(rom, game) and starts execution.
- A cooperative setTimeout loop runs 8192 DMG cycles every approximately 30 ms.
- The 160x144 2-bpp framebuffer is drawn at 0.75 scale in the top area.
- The lower screen is a touch control layout: D-pad, Select, Start, B, A.
- The physical Bangle button exits an active game back to the ROM library.
- An unsupported instruction or other runtime error stops emulation and shows an error screen.

Important tradeoff:
The current scheduler intentionally prioritizes responsiveness and correctness over speed. It should be expected to run substantially slower than real DMG hardware until optional native acceleration is added.
