Status: Complete

Purpose:
Allow users to remove imported ROMs and reclaim Bangle Storage.

Implemented:
- Each ROM listed by interface.html now has a Delete command.
- The App Loader confirmation protects against accidental deletion.
- Deletion erases the ROM, its gbemu.save.<crc> file when present, and then rewrites gbemu.roms.json.
- tests/interface.test.js executes the inline App Loader script with a small DOM/Util mock and verifies cleanup order and library-index update.

Limit:
- Removal is currently available in the App Loader companion interface, not on the watch library screen.

Next action:
Validate import, launch, save, and delete behavior on a real Bangle.js 2 with a legal ROM.
