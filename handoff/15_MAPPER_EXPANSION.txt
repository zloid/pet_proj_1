Status: Complete

Purpose:
Expand the DMG cartridge set beyond ROM ONLY and MBC1 while preserving explicit limits for unsupported hardware.

Implemented:
- MBC3 cartridge types 0x0f through 0x13 support 7-bit ROM banking and external RAM banking.
- MBC3 RTC behavior and persistence are documented in handoff/17_MBC3_RTC.txt.
- MBC5 cartridge types 0x19 through 0x1e support 9-bit ROM banking and external RAM banking.
- The App Loader importer recognizes the same mapper types as the engine.
- Host tests cover MBC3 ROM/RAM banks, MBC5 high ROM/RAM banks, and importer acceptance.

Explicit limits:
- MBC7, HuC-family mappers, camera hardware, and CGB-only ROMs are not supported.
- MBC5 rumble is ignored without changing the selected RAM bank.

Next action:
Run all supported mapper families on real Bangle.js 2 hardware with legal ROMs, then add the next mapper only from a concrete failing ROM or test program.
