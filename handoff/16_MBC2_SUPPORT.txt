Status: Complete

Purpose:
Support the compact MBC2 cartridge family without treating its internal RAM like ordinary external cartridge RAM.

Implemented:
- Cartridge types 0x05 and 0x06 are recognized by the engine and App Loader importer.
- MBC2 ROM-bank writes honor address bit A8.
- The core allocates 512 bytes of internal 4-bit RAM, mirrors the low 9 address bits, and returns a high nibble of ones on reads.
- MBC2 RAM is included in the existing per-ROM save-data path.
- Core and importer tests cover bank switching, RAM mirroring, nibble masking, RAM disable behavior, and import selection.

Next action:
Use a real Bangle.js 2 to validate all supported mapper families, including the RTC against a synchronized device clock.
