Status: Complete

Purpose:
Support the real-time clock in MBC3 timer cartridges without losing time or cartridge RAM across app exits.

Implemented:
- Cartridge types 0x0f and 0x10 are accepted as MBC3 RTC cartridges.
- RTC registers 0x08 through 0x0c use the MBC3 0-to-1 latch protocol.
- Seconds, minutes, hours, 9-bit day count, halt, and carry state advance from the Bangle system clock.
- RTC saves use a GBRT version-1 binary header, RTC registers, a Unix-second timestamp, and then cartridge RAM.
- Old raw RAM saves still load as cartridge RAM without RTC state.
- The app uses getSaveData/loadSaveData when present and falls back to the earlier raw RAM API.
- Host tests cover latching, elapsed time, persistence, cartridge RAM, halt behavior, and importer acceptance.

Operational limit:
- RTC accuracy depends on the Bangle system clock being synchronized.

Next action:
Run a real Bangle.js 2 RTC cartridge through import, save, app restart, and elapsed-time checks before expanding to special hardware mappers.
